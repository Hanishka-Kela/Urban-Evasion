# Urban Evasion: Safe House Dash

A high-octane 3D driving game where you must escape through a procedurally-generated urban cityscape to reach the safe house while evading relentless police cars. Outmaneuver your pursuers, manage your vehicle's armor, and master the streets to survive.

Navigate the maze of city blocks, use buildings as cover, and reach the green safe house before your wanted level escalates. Drift around corners, manage your speed, and stay ahead of the law in this intense action-driving experience.
